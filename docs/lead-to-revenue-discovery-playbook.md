# RightSense Lead-to-Revenue — Discovery & Paid-Pilot Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook is for Mohan and Surya to convert 99Business and similar MSME conversations into a bounded paid pilot. The objective is not to sell a chatbot. The objective is to prove that enquiry capture, response discipline, lead ownership and follow-up can improve measurable revenue execution.

## 20-minute discovery sequence

### 1. Establish the revenue process — 4 minutes

Ask:

- Where do new enquiries arrive today: WhatsApp, calls, website, referrals, email or marketplaces?
- How many WhatsApp enquiries arrive in a normal week or month?
- Who currently sees the first message?
- What happens when that person is unavailable?
- What system should ultimately hold the lead: CRM, spreadsheet, ERP or another application?

### 2. Quantify the operating gap — 5 minutes

Ask:

- Typical first-response time during business hours and after hours?
- Roughly what percentage of enquiries are captured into the sales system?
- How are leads assigned and how quickly?
- How is follow-up tracked?
- How many enquiries are believed to be missed, delayed or forgotten?
- What is the typical value of a qualified opportunity?

Do not invent a baseline when the customer does not know. Mark it as **measure during pilot**.

### 3. Show the reference flow — 5 minutes

Use `/revenue-agent-demo` and narrate only the relevant steps:

1. Enquiry received.
2. Immediate acknowledgement.
3. Minimum requirement captured.
4. Lead qualified using customer-approved rules.
5. Lead synchronized to the existing system of record.
6. Accountable owner assigned and notified.
7. Meeting or next action scheduled.

Avoid discussing advanced AI features until the prospect agrees this workflow solves a real operating problem.

### 4. Define the proof — 4 minutes

Open `/revenue-agent-demo/discovery` and capture:

- prospect company and contact
- industry
- WhatsApp setup
- CRM / lead system
- enquiry volume
- current response time
- current capture rate
- primary lead-loss gap
- accountable sales owner
- one pilot outcome to prove

Primary KPI set:

- enquiry capture rate
- first-response time
- qualified-lead rate
- assignment within SLA
- follow-up completion
- meetings booked
- qualified pipeline influenced
- won revenue influenced

### 5. Ask for the next commercial step — 2 minutes

Recommended close:

> Based on what you have described, we should not start with a large software project. We can scope one WhatsApp-to-sales workflow, establish the current baseline and prove whether a bounded automation pilot improves capture, response and follow-up. If the proof is positive, we can decide together what should scale.

Ask for a follow-up session with the business owner and the person who owns the CRM / WhatsApp setup.

## Qualification gate

Treat the opportunity as **qualified for a paid-pilot proposal** when at least four of these are true:

- A real lead-loss, response or follow-up problem is acknowledged.
- The prospect receives meaningful WhatsApp enquiry volume.
- There is a named business owner.
- There is a system of record or a willingness to use a simple one.
- The customer is willing to measure a baseline.
- The customer can approve access to the required WhatsApp / CRM integration later.
- There is a meaningful economic value per qualified lead or customer.

## Pilot boundary

Start with:

- one business unit or sales team
- one WhatsApp process
- one CRM / lead repository
- one set of qualification rules
- one accountable owner
- one baseline and target KPI set

Do not include in the first pilot unless necessary for the sale:

- multilingual voice
- complex quote generation
- broad marketing automation
- multi-country rollout
- deep ERP integration
- autonomous pricing or commercial commitments

## Commercial discipline

Do not build live provider integrations simply to demonstrate capability. The synthetic reference demo is the pre-sales asset.

Engineering starts customer-specific integration after:

1. the problem and owner are confirmed,
2. the pilot scope is agreed,
3. commercial approval is received, and
4. access/security expectations are documented.

## 30-day funnel target

- 5 discovery calls
- 3 qualified opportunities
- 2 paid-pilot proposals
- at least 1 paid pilot
- target ₹1 lakh+ initial booked revenue

## Founder review

Every Friday review:

- discovery calls completed
- opportunities qualified
- proposals issued
- booked revenue
- expected gross margin
- repeated vs custom requirements
- delivery dependencies
- whether 99Business is creating a repeatable referral channel

The Day-90 decision remains **GO / REFINE / STOP** based on paying customers, repeatability, margin and channel quality.
